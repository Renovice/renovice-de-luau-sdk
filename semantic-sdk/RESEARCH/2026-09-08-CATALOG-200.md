## Consumer rule

Catalog-only `HIGH_CONFIDENCE` means stable observed name/hash, visible arity,
and result shape. Consumers must keep `UnresolvedReceiver` and opaque argument
meanings unresolved. Only `DEEP_CONTRACT` may supply stronger receiver,
parameter, return, authority, lifetime, or callback semantics.
